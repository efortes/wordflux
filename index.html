Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Wordflux?

Wordflux is a content operating system designed for marketing teams and agencies. It connects to your existing content sources (WordPress blogs, websites, RSS, YouTube channels, and documents), extracts core insights, applies your brand visual identity, and schedules native campaigns directly across social networks and CMS platforms.

How does Wordflux differ from ChatGPT or Claude?

ChatGPT is designed for general content ideation and text generation. Wordflux executes the operational marketing workflow around that content: it extracts angles, applies your exact visual brand kit, renders multi-slide PDF carousels, generates client review links, and schedules posts directly to social networks via official developer APIs.

How does Wordflux connect to social networks?

Wordflux uses official developer APIs rather than browser automation or credential scraping. It integrates directly with the Meta Graph API, Google Business Profile API, YouTube Data API, TikTok for Developers, and Pinterest API.

Can agencies manage multiple clients on Wordflux?

Yes. Wordflux provides multi-brand workspaces that keep client accounts, brand kits, and social connections completely separated. Agencies can send unbranded or white-label review links so clients can approve drafts with one click.
