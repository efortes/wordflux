Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Wordflux?

Wordflux is an end-to-end content distribution engine. It connects to your existing content sources (WordPress blogs, websites, RSS, YouTube channels, and documents), extracts core insights, applies your brand visual identity, and schedules native campaigns directly across social networks and CMS platforms.

How does Wordflux differ from ChatGPT or Claude?

While ChatGPT and Claude generate isolated text in a chat prompt, Wordflux AI Chat is an agentic marketing assistant. It can build multi-day campaigns, generate and attach on-brand images, design multi-slide carousels, and directly schedule posts into your publishing calendar.

How does Wordflux connect to social networks?

Wordflux connects through official developer APIs (Meta Graph API, Google Business Profile API, YouTube Data API, TikTok for Developers, and Pinterest API). It does not use fragile web scrapers or unauthorized automation bots.

Can agencies manage multiple clients on Wordflux?

Yes. Wordflux provides multi-brand workspaces that keep client accounts, brand kits, and social connections completely separated. Agencies can send unbranded or white-label review links so clients can approve drafts with one click.
